# iOS keyword intent audit by locale

This is the sequential Astro search-result audit requested after the US pass. The app is the temporary `Teleprompter Keyword Research` iPhone research app (Astro app ID `100`). A ranked search result shows the query's apparent intent; it does not prove that a particular metadata word caused an app's rank. Scores apply to the full measured query, not to a word split out for the keyword field. Popularity 5 is a low-data signal. All result positions are snapshots from 2026-11-09 unless a later date is stated. The audit records tested candidates as well as selected keywords; a measured phrase is not automatically included in metadata.

**Completed:** en-US (see [US word audit](en-keywords.md)); en-GB; en-CA; en-AU; es-MX; ca; cs; da; de-DE; el; es-ES; fi; fr-FR; hi; hr; hu; id; it; ja; ko; ms; nl-NL; no; pl; pt-BR; pt-PT; ro; ru; sk; sv; th; tr; uk; vi; zh-Hans; zh-Hant; ar-SA; he; sl-SI.

**Still to audit sequentially:** none. All 39 locale listings have been checked against the US baseline or storefront search results.

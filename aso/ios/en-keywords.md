## Follow-up competitor research

For “voice teleprompter,” Voice Teleprompter Lite ranked first (2.9 stars, 42 ratings), VoicePrompter: Teleprompter ranked second (4.5 stars, 73 ratings), and Teleprompter.com ranked third (4.8 stars, 30,217 ratings). For “ai teleprompter,” AI Teleprompter: Prompa ranked first (4.6 stars, 23 ratings), and Slate AI: Video Teleprompter ranked second (4.7 stars, 783 ratings). These are US store snapshots, not guarantees of ranking opportunity.

The follow-up measured 44 additional variations. “Voice prompter” is particularly relevant if the product supports voice-based prompting. Rehearsal, memorization, and AI writing keywords are relevant only if the app supports those uses. Many longer voice-control variants scored popularity 5; “voice scroll” scored 19, below the requested threshold.

## US keyword-field intent audit (2026-11-09)

Astro `search_app_store` was checked for all 13 words in the previous en-US keyword field and for the relevant combined queries below. The evidence is **which apps appeared for the query**, not proof that a given competitor ranked because that word appears in its keyword field. Astro's competitor-extraction output supplied additional ideas, but its word-popularity figures alone were not treated as intent evidence. Query scores are for the full phrase, never the isolated component word. A popularity of 5 is a weak/low-data signal.

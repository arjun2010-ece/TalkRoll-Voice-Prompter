# macOS localized keyword research

Astro temporary app **TalkRoll macOS Keyword Research** (ID `102`), `platform=mac`, 2026-11-09. Each locale below uses the named storefront as a proxy; storefronts and metadata languages are not interchangeable. All scores below were measured on the Mac platform. The iOS research supplied candidate spellings only. A popularity of 5 is low data, not proof of traffic or a likely rank. A measured score does not establish search intent: inspect the Mac result snapshot and [word-by-word intent audit](locale-intent-audit.md) before reuse.

The Mac build floats over other apps and supports voice-following scripts, auto-scroll, narration playback, and Mac presentation placement. It does **not** record video in-app. The tables include rejected discovery terms so a high score is never silently treated as an endorsement. [Final proposed metadata](app-store-listings.md) uses product truth and search intent to make the selection.

In several non-English storefronts, the English category query `teleprompter` scored higher and returned more prompter apps than the local spelling. The proposed visible name therefore sometimes keeps this established category loanword, while the subtitle and lower-data feature coverage stay native. This is a storefront-specific choice, not a claim that all users search in English.

Competitor extraction was also sampled for UK `teleprompter`, Japanese `カンペ`, Simplified Chinese `提词器`, and Brazilian Portuguese `teleprompter`. It produced ideas rather than evidence of a ranking cause. We kept only product-relevant ideas after a separate Mac query and SERP check; generic `AI`, captions, pricing, competitor names, and recording claims were excluded.

| Extracted storefront/seed | Useful ideas to verify | Rejected examples |
|---|---|---|
| UK `teleprompter` | `video`, `voice`, `script` | `ai`, `premium`, generic `app` |
| Japan `カンペ` | The extraction itself was mostly low-quality; use measured native category queries instead | `字幕`, `テロップ`, `captions`, `lite` |
| China `提词器` | `语音` as a voice-following clue; measured `悬浮提词器` is stronger category evidence | `字幕`, `offer`, `lite` |
| Brazil `teleprompter` | `voz`, `texto`, `câmera` as feature/category clues | `ai`, `reels`, `grátis` |

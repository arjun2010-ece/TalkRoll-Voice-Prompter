# macOS English keyword research — US

Astro temporary app **TalkRoll macOS Keyword Research** (ID `102`), `platform=mac`, US storefront, 2026-11-09. These are Mac scores and Mac search results; the iPhone scores in `aso/ios/` were not copied. Popularity/difficulty describe a query, not a word extracted from it or a predicted TalkRoll rank.

## Decision summary

- Category anchors: `teleprompter` (63/21), `voice prompter` (39/5), and `voice teleprompter` (34/5) are direct Mac prompter searches. The user-selected name remains `TalkRoll: Voice Prompter`.
- Mac positioning: `notch teleprompter` (5/5), `floating teleprompter` (5/9), `webcam teleprompter` (5/9), and `teleprompter overlay` (5/13) returned directly comparable Mac prompters. Their low popularity is weak demand evidence, but their intent fits the actual floating Mac window.
- Mixed or adjacent: `script` (45/17) and `script reader` (36/13) include screenwriting/annotation apps; `script rehearser` (33/9) is mostly actor rehearsal. Use script words only with prompter context. `teleprompter for video` (55/15) fits using a prompter alongside video tools, but the Mac app does not record video itself.
- Rejected as primary intent: `auto scroll` (33/11) returned general scrolling utilities; `meeting notes` (30/41) returned note takers; `speaker notes` (5/19) returned voice memo/audio apps. Mac notes are a separate reference area, so do not imply floating note-taking or a system recorder.

# macOS locale intent audit

Astro Mac storefront snapshot, 2026-11-09. Temporary research app: **TalkRoll macOS Keyword Research** (ID `102`). Each keyword word below was searched alone and with teleprompter context in that locale. The top results demonstrate intent, not that a word caused a competitor to rank. `Category-title hits` is a conservative count of the first ten app names that explicitly contain a prompter/category term; inspect the listed app names when the count is low. Full Mac scores are in [English research](en-keywords.md) and [localized research](localized-keywords.md).

Product filter: the Mac build has a floating, movable prompter window, voice-following, auto-scroll, script editing/cues, mirror mode, and a separate notes area. It does not record video in-app. We reject screen-recording, generated-caption, AI-writing, generic note-taking, and text-to-speech intent as primary targets.
